## 🇨🇳 中文

把服装参考图、服装概念或穿搭 brief 整理成可重复执行的时装场景展示视频流程，而不是一次性 prompt，适用于 Lookbook 和服装短片。全流程先创建并锁定 Final_Video_Spec（片名、画幅比例、目标时长与意图、视觉风格基调、输出语言、Runway 或 Lifestyle 拍摄路线），路线一旦锁定后续步骤不得重判。STEP 1 从服装图或参考视频中结构化提取廓形、面料、色彩与动态物理（摆动惯性、垂坠方式、反光特征）。STEP 2 分阶段确认服装资产：先生成只有正面/侧面/背面的服装刀版三视图，确认后才以刀版图为参考生成模特服饰卡，多套服装或多角色按 A/B/C 分别生成，场景候选先用文字呈现。之后进入场景选择、分镜绑定、六宫格分镜预览、单套 30 秒视频、BGM 与最终组装。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 服装参考图/穿搭 brief → 可重复执行的时装展示流程，先锁 Final_Video_Spec（片名、画幅、时长、风格基调、语言、拍摄路线）
- Runway 与 Lifestyle 两条路线按服装类型当场锁死：高定礼服概念服走秀场感，休闲街头日常保暖走生活感，歧义不留到后续阶段
- STEP 1 结构化提取廓形（领口/袖型/裙摆裤型）、面料（质地/光泽/透明度/垂坠）、色彩与动态物理（摆动惯性、垂坠方式、反光）
- STEP 2A 先出横版三格服装刀版三视图：无模特、无场景背景，只展示结构并强调缝线、开合、版型、图案与工艺细节
- STEP 2B 确认后才生成模特服饰卡：左侧面部特写/半身肖像观察骨相妆容发型，右侧正侧背全身三视图，保留垂坠折叠与反光
- 流程后半段覆盖场景候选、分镜绑定、六宫格分镜预览、单套 30 秒视频短片、BGM 与最终组装

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns garment references, garment concepts or a styling brief into a repeatable fashion showcase video workflow rather than a one-off prompt, built for lookbooks and fashion shorts. The flow first creates and locks a Final_Video_Spec — project title, aspect ratio, target duration and intent, visual style tone, output language and a Runway or Lifestyle shooting route — and once the route is locked later steps may not re-decide it. Step 1 extracts structured garment information from images or reference video: silhouette, fabric, colour and motion physics such as swing inertia, drape and reflection behaviour. Step 2 confirms apparel assets in stages: it first produces a technical flat three-view (front/side/back only), and only after confirmation generates the model garment card from that flat as reference, with multi-look or multi-character sets generated in A/B/C order and scene candidates presented in text first. It then moves through scene selection, storyboard binding, a six-panel storyboard preview, a 30-second single-look video, BGM and final assembly.

### ✨ What it does

- Garment reference or styling brief → a repeatable fashion showcase workflow, starting by locking the Final_Video_Spec (title, aspect ratio, duration, style tone, language, shooting route)
- Runway and Lifestyle routes are locked on the spot from garment type: couture, gowns and concept wear for the runway feel; casual, street and everyday layering for the lifestyle feel, with ambiguity resolved immediately
- Step 1 extracts silhouette (neckline, sleeve, hem/trouser shape), fabric (texture, sheen, transparency, weight, drape), colour, and motion physics such as swing inertia, drape behaviour and reflection
- Step 2A first outputs a landscape three-panel technical flat: no model, no scene background, structure only, emphasising seams, closures, cut, pattern and craft detail
- Step 2B generates the model garment card only after confirmation — portrait close-up on the left for bone structure, make-up and hair, front/side/back full body on the right, preserving drape, folds and reflection
- The back half of the flow covers scene candidates, storyboard binding, a six-panel storyboard preview, a 30-second single-look video, BGM and final assembly
